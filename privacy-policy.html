Badminton Analyzer (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is designed with privacy as its default. All video analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple Core ML and the Neural Engine. We do not collect, upload, transmit, or store your videos or any personal information.

No account No tracking No ads No analytics SDK Works offline

1. What data we process

The App processes only the badminton video you explicitly choose to analyze:

  • Photo library access (read): the App reads only the single video you select from your library. It never scans or browses the rest of your library.
  • On-device inference: pose estimation, shuttlecock tracking, and stroke classification are performed locally with models bundled inside the App. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Exported video (write): an annotated video is saved to your photo library only when you tap the Save button.
  • Built-in demo: a sample clip is bundled with the App for testing. It contains no real personal data.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or identifiers.
  • We do not upload your videos, analysis results, or statistics.
  • We do not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • There is no sign-in and no account.

3. Where your data is stored

Your source videos remain in your photo library, and exported videos are saved there by you. During analysis, temporary frames are processed in the App’s private memory and are discarded. We operate no server and retain nothing.

4. Permissions

  • Photo Library: used to read the video you choose and to save annotated videos when you request it. You can revoke this at any time in the iOS Settings app.

5. Third-party components

The App uses open-source AI components that run entirely on-device (e.g., RTMO/MMPose under the Apache License 2.0, and TrackNetV3). These components do not communicate over the network and do not collect data. See the Open Source Notices in the App / on this site.

6. Children

The App is rated for ages 4+, contains no advertising, and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no server-side profile to access, export, or delete. All of your content remains under your control on your device. If applicable under the GDPR, CCPA, or your local law, you may contact us for any privacy request.

8. Changes to this policy

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
